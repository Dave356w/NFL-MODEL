# Market-only relationships and rolling residuals

Protocol frozen before execution, 2026-10-10. Research only; exposed development. Stage one uses only schedule market moneylines, spread/total lines and both-side spread/total prices, plus actual earlier outcomes. No box-score features or categorical team coefficients. Labels: actual home-minus-away margin and combined points. Market centers: spread_line (positive home favorite) and total_line.

Compare market-centered benchmark, market_relationships and four rolling-residual corrections: half-life 4 or 16 team games, zero-prior pseudo-games 4 or 12, max three prior seasons with .5 offseason retention. Team margin residuals are from that team's perspective; total residual belongs to a game and enters each participating team's history, but each game remains one training observation. Shrunk mean = sum(w*r)/(sum(w)+K); margin adds home-minus-away profile, total adds average profiles. All histories exclude own/current/later weeks; sample/support metadata saved.

Margin price features: spread/10, fair moneyline logit, centered total (line-45)/10, spread*centered total, fair cover logit. Total price features: centered total, absolute spread/10, absolute fair moneyline logit, spread squared, fair over logit. Same eligible priced games for all arms. Both targets use fixed mean ridge .1, unpenalized intercept, target/10 and strictly prior fitted feature scales. Gaussian integer-bin scoring and prequential dispersion calibration reused from the previous experiment, with common earlier-market-error fallback. No target/variance grid or ROI optimization.

Generate weekly OOF forecasts from 2020 onward. At each season boundary, select relationships or one of the four residual rules separately for margin and total by preceding seasons' OOF integer-bin negative log likelihood. Use relationships for initial 2020 warm-up. Selections are frozen for that season. Report 2023–25 selected forecasts versus market-centered benchmark and fixed relationships, plus all fixed candidates. Primary selected-vs-market and selected-vs-relationships NLL contrasts across two targets use 98.75% paired week-cluster intervals. Fixed candidates are diagnostics; do not treat their best retrospective rank as qualified. Report CRPS, MAE/RMSE, interval coverage and cover/over LL versus actual two-sided no-vig prices; pushes excluded identically.

Stack only each game's honestly selected pregame forecasts. Compare production matched to the same OOF history, forecast_additive, margin_total, pass_total, pass_market_total using the established definitions in margin_total_models.py. Primary four candidate-vs-production contrasts use 98.75% intervals. Also compare candidates with full-history production; source and interaction attribution contrasts are descriptive 95%. Select one of these five win models annually by earlier seasons' binary LL, never by ROI. Report units, ROI +/-SE, market-null, same-row favorite, LL/Brier, annual performance and matched/full history difference. Also report direct selected/relationships margin-derived conditional non-tie winner probabilities.
