# Direct margin/total models and production interactions

Protocol frozen before execution, 2026-10-10. Research only; production v1.16 and forward ledger untouched. History is exposed development.

Targets: home minus away score (margin) and home plus away score (total). Each has market-centered benchmark, market + correction, and standalone mean model. Fixed weighted ridge .1, unpenalized intercept, target divided by 10, feature scaling from strictly earlier training. Margin uses production main differences and passing product/hinges; total uses sums of raw h8 offense/allowed profiles, summed availability and peaks, excluding sack rates as production does. Features are prior-game ratios and roster-aware inputs reconstructed with the existing recipe. Market centers are schedule spread_line (positive home favored) and total_line; lines are location proxies, not necessarily expected means.

Predictive approximation: Gaussian location distributions, with integer-bin probabilities for observed point scores and pushes. Forecast dispersion is estimated from each arm's strictly earlier weekly out-of-sample residuals; before sufficient residuals exist, use earlier actual-minus-market-line residuals as common fallback. Weighted RMS around the specified forecast center, floor 3 points. Negative total support is a disclosed approximation, not a coherent joint NFL score model. No mean/variance selection grid.

Fit weekly beginning 2020; evaluate target scores 2023–25 and show annual results. Log score is negative log integer-bin probability; CRPS of underlying Gaussian is secondary, alongside MAE/RMSE and interval coverage. At market lines, exclude pushes from BOTH binary log-loss arms and compare model conditional nonpush cover/over probability against actual two-sided no-vig prices. Report push counts; odds-conditional distributions do not identify market push mass. Spread and total prices/lines are schedule quotes with undocumented snapshot times, not native forward lock prices. Full-distribution baseline is explicitly market-centered, not a fully observed market distribution.

Stage-two production uses only forecasts generated before their own games. Begin stack fitting in 2022, evaluate 2023–25; train on identical eligible binary games with priced OOF features in all arms. Compare matched-cohort production, forecast_additive, margin_total and pass_total:
- margin_edge = (corrected margin mean - market margin line)/10
- total_context = (corrected total mean - 45)/10
- forecast_additive adds margin_edge and site*total_context to the original recipe
- margin_total additionally adds margin_edge*total_context
- pass_total additionally adds pass_product*total_context
Original signed feature effects and added total-homefield term are retained; no arbitrary probability blend. All coefficients refit jointly with production ridge/scaling/market-logit offset. Warm-up reduces stage-two training history; quantify matched production versus full-history production to disclose that cost.

Primary fixed forecast source is market-corrected, unchanged by retrospective score rankings. Also annually select one of the four stage-two arms using strictly earlier-season win log loss (2022 for first 2023 selection, 2022–23 for 2024, etc.). No ROI selection. Report flat 1u moneyline units/ROI ±SE, market-null, same-row favorite, LL/Brier. Fixed three production contrasts use paired 98.333% week-cluster intervals; selected 95%. Distribution contrasts and event LL gains use 98.75% intervals for the four fixed corrected/standalone contrasts over two targets. Supplemental diagnostics added after the initial run, without new model fitting choices: direct-margin win scores and paired comparisons against additive/full production. Also add a source ablation, pass_market_total: same margin_edge but replace forecast total_context by (market total_line -45)/10 in the homefield and passing-product terms. Compare pass_total against it at descriptive 95%; keep this supplemental arm out of the original annual selector. This isolates whether the predicted total contributes beyond the market total. These are descriptive, not pre-registered confirmations. Interaction-vs-additive comparisons use 97.5% intervals for two contrasts; the three new arms also compare directly with full-history production at 98.333%. Derived direct-margin conditional non-tie win probabilities get same-row moneyline diagnostics. No intervals establish pristine confirmation or include design-selection uncertainty.
