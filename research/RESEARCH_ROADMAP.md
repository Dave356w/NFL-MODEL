R002 gain is strongest in 2024; excluding that season leaves +0.004710, below
its pooled practical threshold. Conditional winner/tie probabilities do not
improve, and team NLL/CRPS and total CRPS slightly worsen. A future review must
keep these limitations visible. The score correction is not an inferred OT law.

R003's positive exact-score effect follows conditioning on valid support and
is too small under its fixed gate. Do not extend masks, increase strength or
select favorable score slices after seeing these results. Any new scoring-
frequency model receives a new ID and explicitly fresh evaluation.

Data acquisition for a mechanistic model must record regulation score, OT flag,
period/possession increments, source revisions and available-at times. Validate
that regulation plus OT increments equals final scores and preserve rule-era
separation. Do not substitute final winning margin for an observed OT increment.

Keep original rejected residual variants and current negative secondary metrics.
Preserve output failures separately from candidate revisions. No automation,
provider connection, deployment or ledger mutation is authorized by this roadmap.
