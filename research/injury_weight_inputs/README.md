# Frozen saved-injury replay inputs

Two different evidence definitions are kept separate:

- `saved_vectors_2026.csv`: 72 completed games with the latest eligible saved
  model-input vector generated by T60. These include roster-departure absence
  proxies and may precede the final injury/inactive update.
- `saved_reports_2026.csv`: 12 completed games with both teams' latest pre-T60
  complete/issue-free official reports. Medical-only snap burdens are rebuilt
  from their statuses and strictly earlier team snaps. This is not an exact
  native execution replay; original snap-source publication times are unverified.
- `selected_reports.jsonl`: the 24 selected source-report records, copied into
  research storage; original journals are not modified.
- `historical_vectors.csv` / `historical_medical.csv`: respectively 816 and 783
  reconstructed 2023–25 training games. Medical training excludes 33 games with
  a nonfinal/missing team-report state.
- `coverage_2026.csv`: every graded 2026 game and both evidence eligibility flags.
  Missing reports/vectors are excluded rather than replaced with healthy values.
- `provenance.json`: source hashes and historical timing limitations.

The stricter latest-report check blocks GB-NE after a newer incomplete report;
this reduces the initial 13 complete-report count to 12 without using its result.

Reproduce: `python research/positional_injury_weights.py`. `--prepare` rebuilds
from existing source archives and cached prior snaps, without network collection.
New upstream/source revisions may change hashes and eligibility.

This study fits injury-to-margin residual corrections. It does not rebuild team
passing/rushing/first-down efficiency profiles under a changed starting lineup.
